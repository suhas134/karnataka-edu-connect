Division 01

Bengaluru Division

ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು ವಿಭಾಗ

India's Silicon Valley and premier higher education capital. Home to iconic degree & GFGCs, autonomous VTU engineering institutions (RVCE, BMSCE, MSRIT, DSCE, BIT), medical giants (BMCRI, St. John's), AYUSH & Ayurvedic hospitals (GAMC, Sri Sri, SDM), and NLSIU Law.

9 Districts: Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural, Ramanagara, Tumakuru, Shivamogga, Davanagere, Kolar, Chikkaballapura, Chitradurga.

Division 02

Belagavi Division

ಬೆಳಗಾವಿ ವಿಭಾಗ

North-Western Karnataka's technical and cultural capital. Headquarters of VTU, historic engineering foundations (BEC Bagalkot), medical giants, and heritage universities.

7 Districts: Belagavi, Bagalkot, Dharwad, Vijayapura, Gadag, Haveri, Uttara Kannada (Karwar).

Division 03

Kalaburagi Division

ಕಲಬುರಗಿ ವಿಭಾಗ (Kalyana Karnataka)

The historical Kalyana Karnataka region entitled to Article 371(J) reservation. Hosts Central University of Karnataka, premier medical hospitals, and autonomous VTU engineering colleges.

7 Districts: Kalaburagi, Bidar, Ballari, Raichur, Koppal, Yadgir, Vijayanagara.
CUK Kalaburagi PDA College (VTU) GNDEC Bidar (VTU) BKIT Bhalki (VTU) RYMEC Ballari (VTU) BITM Ballari (VTU) MR Medical ESIC Medical VIMS Ballari

Division 04

Mysuru Division

ಮೈಸೂರು ವಿಭಾಗ

Celebrated for century-old academic traditions and coastal educational hubs. Home to University of Mysore, SJCE / JSS STU, NIE, VVCE, MCE Hassan, PESCE Mandya, Sahyadri, and NITK Surathkal.

8 Districts: Mysuru, Dakshina Kannada (Mangaluru), Udupi, Hassan, Mandya, Chikkamagaluru, Chamarajanagar, Kodagu.

Admissions Roadmap

Karnataka Entrance Exams & Portals

Official centralized portals governing admissions to colleges across all 4 Karnataka divisions.

KCET (KEA Karnataka)

kea.kar.nic.in

For B.E. (RVCE, SJCE, BEC, PDA, SIT, NIE), B.Pharm, B.Sc Nursing, and Agriculture. Conducted annually with 3 rounds of counseling.

NEET UG / MCC

National / KEA State

Mandatory for MBBS (BMCRI, MMCRI, VIMS, SNMC, MRMC), BDS (PMNM), and BAMS with centralized seat allotment.

UUCMS Karnataka Portal

uucms.karnataka.gov.in

Unified online single-window admission portal for all general degree programs (BA, B.Com, B.Sc, BCA, BBA, MSW) in Government and Aided Colleges.

Article 371(J) Portal

Kalyana Karnataka Quota

Special constitutional reservations (8% statewide & 70% regional quota) for students native to Kalaburagi Division districts.
